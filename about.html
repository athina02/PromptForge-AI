About PromptForge AI

PromptForge AI is a free AI prompt library that helps users discover high-quality prompts for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other AI tools.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make AI easier and more productive by providing ready-to-use prompts for everyone.

What We Offer

  • Hundreds of AI prompts
  • Easy search
  • Categories
  • One-tap copy
  • Favorites
  • Progressive Web App (PWA)

Who Is It For?

Students, developers, creators, businesses, marketers and anyone using AI.

Privacy

We respect your privacy and never sell your personal information.

Our Vision

We want PromptForge AI to become one of the world's largest free AI prompt libraries.
